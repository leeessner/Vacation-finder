Every resort in config/stays.yaml is here. For each one: what a stay there looks like day by day, the outings worth leaving the resort for, which city it pairs with, and whether it fits the profile. The profile's test is "a whole nature experience, pool plus guided outdoors, never just a pool."

How to use a resort. Five nights is the sweet spot. Spend two days at the resort (pool, kids' club, the resort's own guided hike), two outings (castle, mine, train, gorge), and one slow day. The resort plans meals and childcare, which is the point. Pair it with a city of 4–5 nights, before or after.

Prices. None of these can be tracked on Google; they sell direct by season and child age. The one real figure so far is ULRICHSHOF, at about €713 a night all-inclusive for two adults and two children (July 2026). For six, budget roughly €1,000–1,300 a night until a quote says otherwise, and ask every resort with all four ages. Outings below are from tourism sites and family-travel posts. Anything marked check hasn't been confirmed for summer 2027.

Austria

Dachsteinkönig Familux Resort — Gosau, Salzkammergut

All-inclusive. Childcare from 7 days old. Outdoor play area, petting zoo. The plan is in munich-bavaria-resort.md. It covers the Hallstatt salt mine (miners' slides, underground lake; ages 4+), the Gosausee lakes loop, the Gosaukamm cable car, Hohensalzburg fortress on the way in, and optionally Hohenwerfen's falconry show. - Pairs with: Munich (2½–3 h) or Salzburg (1 h). - Fit: ★★★ It meets the profile's test: mountains, lakes, a castle and a mine that's unique, not generic.

Familien Natur Resort Moar Gut — Grossarl, Salzburger Land

Five-star family resort on its own organic farm. Family suites only. - Day at the resort: the farm (a bonus, not the point), pools, guided family hike on the Grossarl valley's alpine pastures. Ask what the resort runs each week. - Outing 1, castle: Hohenwerfen (about 40 min). A cliff-top fortress with an armoury and a falconry show (€23.90 adult, €9.10 child 6–14, including the lift). - Outing 2, gorge: the Liechtensteinklamm (about 25 min). Walkways and a spiral stair through a deep rock gorge; stroller-free. Check it's open, since it has closed for repairs in the past. - Option, ice cave: Eisriesenwelt in Werfen, the world's largest ice cave (€41 adult online, €22 child 5–14). It's 700 steps and cold, and the cave itself says it's often too much for 3–4-year-olds. Older kids and one parent only. - Option, city: Salzburg (1 h): Hohensalzburg by funicular, then the Hellbrunn trick fountains (check). - Pairs with: Munich (2½ h) or Salzburg. - Fit: ★★★

Naturhotel Forsthofgut — Leogang, Salzburger Land

Half board (per listings). Rocky's Kids Club. Forest spa. Chalet suites. - Day at the resort: forest spa, the kids' club, a guided forest walk. - Outing 1, mountain: the Leogang cable car to the Asitz summit playgrounds and themed kids' trails (check summer operation). - Outing 2, waterfall: the Krimml Waterfalls, the highest in Austria (about 1¼ h). Paths are steep in places but doable in short sections. - Outing 3, city and castle: Salzburg (1 h), Hohensalzburg. - Pairs with: Munich (2½ h) or Salzburg. - Fit: ★★ It's lovely, but half board means planning lunches; it's the "design" pick of the round-up.

Familienresort Buchau — Maurach, Lake Achensee, Tyrol

All-inclusive. Directly on the lake. Childcare 6 days a week. - Day at the resort: lake beach, pools, kids' club. - Outing 1, train: the Achensee steam cog railway, from 1889 and one of Europe's oldest, from Jenbach up to the lake (check the 2027 season). - Outing 2, castle: Ambras Castle, Innsbruck (about 45 min). A Renaissance Habsburg castle with a Chamber of Art and Curiosities and an armoury. Combine it with Innsbruck's old town and Golden Roof. - Outing 3, the unusual: Rattenberg, a tiny medieval glass-blowing town (about 30 min). Watch glass being made, the profile's "making-of" kind of stop. - Pairs with: Munich (1½ h, the closest resort to Munich). - Fit: ★★ Good, but the lake beach and boats are the centre of it, and the family isn't big on boats.

Italy — South Tyrol and Trentino

South Tyrol is half Austrian and half Italian. The food is Italian pasta and Austrian dumplings, which suits the kids and the vegetarian parent (cheese dumplings, Schlutzkrapfen spinach ravioli).

Das Mühlwald — Quality Time Family Resort — Natz-Schabs, near Brixen

Full board or all-inclusive package (listings differ). All-day childcare. Petting zoo. Family spa. - Day at the resort: spa, childcare, the plateau's walking paths. - Outing 1, old town: Brixen/Bressanone (15 min): the cathedral cloister frescoes and the prince-bishops' Hofburg. Walk next door to Neustift Abbey, an Augustinian monastery with its own winery. - Outing 2, the iceman: the South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology in Bolzano (45 min), home of Ötzi, the 5,300-year-old glacier mummy. It's unique in the world, so it passes the profile's "only if unique" test. - Outing 3, Dolomites: the Seiser Alm gondola (€30 adult return; kids born 2018 or later ride free). It goes up to Europe's largest high alpine meadow, with flat walks, huts for lunch and the Dolomites all around. - Outing 4, castle: Rodenegg Castle (20 min), with the oldest secular frescoes in the German-speaking world (check tour times). - Pairs with: Venice or Verona (2½ h), or Munich over the Brenner (3 h). - Fit: ★★★

SAN SONNEA Family Dolomites (formerly Sonnwies) — Lüsen, near Brixen

Family-only. Working farm with horses. Pool and spa. Reviewers call it all-inclusive (check). - The same outings as Das Mühlwald (Brixen, Ötzi, Seiser Alm, Rodenegg), since it's 20 minutes away. - At the resort: pony and horse time, a bonus, and the farm. - Fit: ★★★ Choose between this and Das Mühlwald on the quote and the suite layout for six.

Familienhotel Huber — Vals, Gitschberg Jochtal

All-inclusive, alcohol-free. Indoor pool with waterslide. Daily childcare. Guided family hikes. - At the resort: the hotel's own guided family hikes are exactly what the profile asks for. The Jochtal gondola goes to kids' adventure trails (check). - Outings: Brixen and Neustift Abbey (25 min), Ötzi (1 h), Rodenegg. - Fit: ★★ It's good. Alcohol-free all-inclusive means parents pay for wine and beer, so Austria's beer-garden culture is a drive away.

Feuerstein Nature Family Resort — Pflersch valley, near the Brenner Pass

All-inclusive offered. 70 hours a week of Montessori-based nature childcare. 1,250 m altitude. Woodworking, mud room, climbing. - At the resort: the nature childcare programme is the guided outdoor experience, with woodworking and climbing for the older two. - Outing 1, old town: Sterzing/Vipiteno (20 min), a pastel medieval main street with a clock tower. - Outing 2, city and castle: Innsbruck (45 min) and Ambras Castle. - Outing 3: Ötzi in Bolzano (1 h). - Pairs with: Munich (2½ h) or Verona (2½ h). - Fit: ★★★ It's the most "outdoors" of the list.

Garberhof Dolomit Family — Rasen-Antholz, Pustertal

The hotel says all-inclusive (one listing shows half board). Kids' world, pools. - Outing 1, lake: Lago di Braies/Pragser Wildsee (40 min). A turquoise lake with a flat 1-hour loop. Go early, and in summer you must book access by car (check the rules). - Outing 2, castle: Bruneck Castle (25 min), now one of Messner's Mountain Museums (check the price). - Outing 3: the Antholz lake and biathlon centre (15 min). - Pairs with: Venice (2½ h). - Fit: ★★

Gardea SoulFamily Resort — Torbole, Lake Garda

Board not confirmed. Two pools with waterslide. On the lake, not in the mountains. The round-up called it a Montessori mountain resort, but research places it on Lake Garda and the Montessori childcare at Feuerstein. Worth confirming with the account. - Outing 1, water castle: Sirmione's Scaliger Castle, a moated castle on the lake (1 h). - Outing 2, mountain: the Malcesine–Monte Baldo cable car, with rotating cabins (40 min). - Outing 3, city: Verona (1¼ h): the Roman Arena and the old town. - Pairs with: Venice, Verona or Milan. - Fit: ★ It's a lake-and-pool resort. Against the profile's "never just a pool" test, it's the weakest.

Switzerland

Märchenhotel Braunwald — Glarus

Half board, with a separate children's dining room in the evening. Supervised childcare 12 hours a day. A fairy-tale theme. In a car-free village reached by funicular. - At the resort: the funicular is the arrival, which is trains points before you've checked in. Walk the car-free village's trails; there are mountain paths straight from the door. - Outing 1, lake: the Klöntalersee (45 min), a flat lakeshore walk. - Outing 2, unique museum: the Swiss Museum of Transport in Lucerne (1½ h). Trains, cable cars and planes; one of the profile's "unique" science-type exceptions. Combine it with Lucerne's covered Chapel Bridge. - Pairs with: Zurich or Lucerne. Switzerland is expensive, so the food line roughly doubles. - Fit: ★★ It's charming. The cost is the question.

Germany (Bavaria)

ULRICHSHOF Nature · Family · Design — Rimbach, Bavarian Forest

All-inclusive with non-alcoholic drinks all day. Family spa and a separate parents' spa. Outdoor forest pool. Pony camp. About €713 a night for two adults and two children (July 2026). - At the resort: forest pool, pony camp (a bonus), spa. - Outing 1, treetops: the Bavarian Forest National Park treetop walk at Neuschönau (about 1 h). An egg-shaped tower above the trees, stroller-friendly ramps. - Outing 2, city: Regensburg (1 h): a medieval old town, the stone bridge, the cathedral, and the 500-year-old Historic Sausage Kitchen for the meat-eaters. - Outing 3, beer: Bavarian Forest breweries. Ask the resort which ones welcome families. - Pairs with: Munich (2 h) or Prague (2½ h), which would be a new city for the list. - Fit: ★★ It's the one resort with a real price, and it's good value. The outings are gentler than the Alps.

Oberjoch Familux Resort — Bad Hindelang, Allgäu

Out for summer 2027. It's being rebuilt and reopens in December 2027. It would otherwise have paired perfectly with Neuschwanstein (30 min away). Revisit for 2028.
