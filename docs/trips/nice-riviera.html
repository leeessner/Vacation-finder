Nice & the French Riviera

Shape: 9 nights in one apartment in Nice, with every day trip by train. This is the beach trip: the French Riviera is the profile's own example of "a beach different from home". Each beach day is paired with an old town, a fort or a castle, and there's a narrow-gauge mountain train to a walled citadel.

Matches the tracked trip "Nice & the French Riviera". Written against plans/family-profile.md. Prices were checked in October 2026; anything not confirmed is marked check. Euros are converted at about $1.15 per €1.

Where Nights Getting around

Fly into and out of Nice (NCE). The tram runs from the airport to the centre.

Day by day

Day 1 — Arrive. Walk the Promenade des Anglais and have the first swim. Nice's beaches are pebbles, so bring water shoes. Book a beach-club section with loungers for easy days.

Day 2 — Old Nice and Castle Hill. The Cours Saleya flower and food market. Then take the free lift or the steps up the Colline du Château, the hill where Nice's castle stood. It has ruins, a waterfall, a big playground, and the best view of the Baie des Anges. Go to the beach in the afternoon.

Day 3 — Monaco. Take the train (20 min). See the Prince's Palace changing of the guard (just before noon; check the time), then the old town on the Rock. The Oceanographic Museum on the cliff edge is optional; it's an aquarium (a bonus, not a goal).

Day 4 — Villefranche. Take the train 7 minutes to one of the coast's best family beaches. It's sandier and calmer, in a deep bay. Explore the citadel and the vaulted medieval street, Rue Obscure.

Day 5 — The Train des Pignes. The narrow-gauge "pine-cone train" climbs from Nice into the mountains, to Entrevaux. It's a walled medieval town with a drawbridge, and a citadel reached by a zig-zag ramp up the cliff. This is the trains-and-castles day. Part of the line reopened in August 2026 after works (check the 2027 timetable). Summer steam trains run on some days on the same line.

Day 6 — Beach day. A slow day at the beach club.

Day 7 — Antibes. See Fort Carré, the star-shaped fort on the point, and the walled old town and Provençal market. Swim at Plage de la Gravette, a sandy beach inside the ramparts.

Day 8 — Île Sainte-Marguerite. Take the train to Cannes, then a 15-minute boat to the island. In the Fort Royal, the Man in the Iron Mask was imprisoned. The island has pine woods and little coves; the kids will remember the cell. It's a short boat ride, but it is a boat. Or go to Èze, the medieval "eagle's nest" village on a rock above the sea.

Day 9 — Beach day, or a second choice from Day 8.

Day 10 — Fly home from NCE.

Food — and protein for the vegetarian parent

Nice's street food happens to be chickpeas: - Socca, a crisp chickpea pancake from the wood oven, and panisse, chickpea fries. Both are kid favourites. - Also salade niçoise (egg, tuna), pan bagnat, pissaladière and ratatouille. Petits farcis are stuffed vegetables; ask whether they're made without meat. - Fish for the others: grilled daurade and bouillabaisse further west. - Kids' backup: pizza (Nice does it well), socca, pasta.

Rosé for the parents. Nice has a few craft breweries, but this isn't a beer trip.

Watch out for

  • Pebble beaches. Bring water shoes for everyone.
  • July and August crowds. June is calmer, and the sea is already warm enough.
  • Monaco is pricey. Eat in Nice.
