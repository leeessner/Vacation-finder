Milan & Lake Como

Shape: 9 nights. Four in Milan, Leonardo's city: The Last Supper, his machines and notebooks, Raphael's School of Athens cartoon, a castle with Michelangelo's last sculpture, and a walk on the cathedral roof. Then five on Lake Como in Varenna: a castle with falcons, lakeside villas, a funicular, and swimming.

Day by day

Day 0 — Travel day: fly overnight. Leave St. Louis in the afternoon with one connection (usually Chicago, Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas or an East Coast hub). Fly overnight, and land the next morning, which is Day 1. Allow about 11–15 hours gate to gate. The clocks jump 7 hours ahead, so Day 1 is for settling in, not sightseeing. The trip page's Flights section shows the airports, connection and times of the fare being tracked.

Day 1 — Arrive. See the Duomo square and the glass-roofed Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II. Spin your heel on the bull mosaic for luck.

Day 2 — The Last Supper, then the castle.Sun Jun 6: Leave 8:40a (metro M1 to Conciliazione) · arrive 9:00a · The Last Supper, your 15-minute slot (be there 20 min early) Your 15-minute slot at Leonardo's Last Supper (under-18s free, but every person needs a reservation). Then go to Castello Sforzesco, the Sforza dukes' castle. It has moats, towers, an armoury, and Michelangelo's unfinished Rondanini Pietà, the sculpture he was working on days before he died. The Sempione park behind the castle has playgrounds.

Day 3 — The Ambrosiana, then the cathedral roof.Mon Jun 7: Leave 9:40a (a short walk) · arrive 9:55a · Pinacoteca Ambrosiana opens 10:00 The Pinacoteca Ambrosiana holds Raphael's full-size cartoon for The School of Athens (Room 7). It also has Caravaggio's Basket of Fruit, Leonardo's Portrait of a Musician, and pages of his notebooks, the Codex Atlanticus. In the afternoon, take the lift to the Duomo rooftop terraces and walk among the spires and statues.

Day 4 — Leonardo's machines, then Brera, then the Roastery.Tue Jun 8: Leave 9:00a (metro M2 to Sant'Ambrogio) · arrive 9:25a · Science museum opens 9:30 (submarine tours fill early) The National Museum of Science and Technology Leonardo da Vinci has models of his machines built from the notebooks. It also has a real submarine, the Enrico Toti, which you can board on a tour (check ages). It's unique, so it passes the profile's test. In the afternoon, Pinacoteca di Brera: Raphael's Marriage of the Virgin, Mantegna's Dead Christ and Caravaggio's Supper at Emmaus. End at the Starbucks Reserve Roastery in Piazza Cordusio, the full roasting experience the profile asks for.

Day 5 — Train to Varenna (1 h). Walk up the old mule path to Castello di Vezio. It's a medieval tower with falconry, with birds of prey trained on site, and a view down the lake.

Day 6 — Bellagio.Thu Jun 10: Leave 9:45a (15-minute crossing) · arrive 10:15a · Ferry to Bellagio (about every 30 min) A 15-minute ferry crossing, short enough for a non-boat family. Explore the stepped lanes and the gardens of Villa Melzi. Swim at the Lido di Varenna in the afternoon.

Day 7 — Villa Carlotta. Short ferry to Tremezzo. It's sculpture and gardens: Canova-school marble (Cupid and Psyche), Thorvaldsen, Hayez's The Last Kiss, and terraced gardens with azaleas and a bamboo grove.

Day 8 — Como and the Brunate funicular. Take the train or ferry to Como, ride the funicular up to Brunate, and walk to the lighthouse for the whole-lake view. See the old walled town below.

Day 9 — The lake, slow. A beach and pool day. Or take the Bernina Express day trip from Tirano over the Alps into Switzerland, the most famous train ride in the Alps (long: about 2 h to Tirano first).

Day 10 — Train to MXP (about 2 h) and fly home. It's a full travel day home: westbound flights usually leave late morning or midday and land in St. Louis that evening, about 12–16 hours gate to gate. You gain back the 7 hours.

Food — and protein for the vegetarian parent

  • Risotto alla milanese; pizzoccheri, buckwheat pasta with cheese, potato and cabbage from the valleys above the lake; polenta with cheese.
  • Plenty of vegetarian choice in Milan. Joia is the famous (Michelin-starred) vegetarian restaurant, for a parents' night.
  • Lake fish for the others: perch (pesce persico) with butter and sage.
  • Kids' backup: pasta, pizza, cotoletta (Milanese schnitzel).

Coffee: the Starbucks Reserve Roastery is on Day 4. Beer: Birrificio Lambrate, Milan's first craft brewery, for the parents.

Watch out for

  • Varenna is steep and stepped. Pick an apartment near the station or the ferry, and bring a carrier for the 3-year-old.
  • The lake is busiest July–August. June is calmer.
  • Ferries are crowded on weekends. Do Bellagio and Villa Carlotta on weekdays.
