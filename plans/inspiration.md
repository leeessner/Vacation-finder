# Inspiration and notes for the trip plans

Everything sent from the family-travel accounts, transcribed so it survives
between conversations. Source posts are Instagram carousels (not readable
automatically), so this file is the record. The plans in this folder are
drafted from these, the house rules in `config/trips.yaml`, and the family's
own answers.

## Answers so far (how we travel)

- Pace: one main activity in the morning; the 3-year-old manages fine, so
  afternoons can hold another activity. No need for many breaks.
- Europe: no car in cities (walk + public transport); car only outside cities.
- Family apartments welcome in European cities (one unit for six).
- Two-base splits are right (e.g. Barcelona 6 + Madrid 4, Athens 3 + island 7).
- No fixed budget.
- Plans must reflect what this family actually likes, learned by interview —
  not a generic "a museum and a coffee shop". Interview still open.

## Portugal

**"5 Days in Lisbon" (Tripist)**
1. Baixa & Alfama — Praça do Comércio, Rua Augusta Arch, Lisbon Cathedral,
   São Jorge Castle, Miradouro de Santa Luzia
2. Belém — Jerónimos Monastery, Belém Tower, Padrão dos Descobrimentos,
   MAAT, Pastéis de Belém
3. Chiado & Bairro Alto — Carmo Convent, Santa Justa Lift, Elevador da Bica,
   Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara
4. Sintra day trip — Pena Palace, Moorish Castle, Quinta da Regaleira,
   Sintra National Palace, Cabo da Roca
5. Modern Lisbon & views — LX Factory, Time Out Market, Parque das Nações,
   Oceanário, Telecabine Lisboa

**"Portugal with Kids — 10 days": Porto 3 → Lisbon 4 → Lagos 3** (by train)
- Porto: Ribeira; Six Bridges river cruise; World of Discoveries; Gaia cable
  car; Crystal Palace Gardens (peacocks); São Bento station tiles
- Lisbon: Oceanário; Belém; Pastéis de Belém; São Jorge Castle; Tram 28;
  Pavilhão do Conhecimento (hands-on science)
- Lagos: Ponta da Piedade by boat; Praia Dona Ana; old town; Meia Praia;
  Lagos Zoo; sunset coast walk

Open question: the tracker assumes Lisbon + the Azores; these posts suggest
Porto–Lisbon–Lagos instead.

## Italy — Tuscany ("Tuscany with Kids", JollyGoodTravel-style carousel)

- **Florence**: stay in Fiesole; Boboli Gardens; the interactive da Vinci
  museum; a pasta cooking class.
- **Fiesole**: stay at **Fattoria di Maiano** — agriturismo 20 min outside
  the city, pool, animals, gardens; family-style apartments. Walk to
  **Cucina Pietraforte** for dinner on a terrace under string lights.
  (In `config/stays.yaml`.)
- **San Gimignano**: skip the queues at the popular towers; climb **Torre
  Salvucci Maggiore**, a private residence open to the public, for views
  over Tuscany.
- **Pienza**: home of pecorino cheese. **La Terrazza del Chiostro**
  (requested).
- **Greve in Chianti**: **Castello di Brolio**, birthplace of modern Chianti
  wine — gardens, a "spooky crypt", lots of space to run. Horseback riding
  through a vineyard nearby.
- **Montecatini Terme**: funicular up to **Montecatini Alto** (requested).
- A "designed for families" agriturismo about 25 min from Florence — name
  still to come.
