# Milan & Lake Como — draft plan (summer 2027)

**Shape:** 9 nights. Four in **Milan**, Leonardo's city: *The Last Supper*,
his machines and notebooks, Raphael's *School of Athens* cartoon, a castle
with Michelangelo's last sculpture, and a walk on the cathedral roof. Then
four on **Lake Como** in Varenna: a castle with falcons, lakeside villas, a
funicular, and swimming. The last night is at a hotel by the airport, so the trip home doesn't start before dawn.

Not tracked yet. Written against `plans/family-profile.md`. Prices were
checked in October 2026; anything not confirmed is marked *check*. Euros are
converted at about **$1.15 per €1**.

## Day by day

**Day 0 — Travel day: fly overnight.** Leave St. Louis in the afternoon with one connection (usually Chicago, Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas or an East Coast hub). Fly overnight, and land the next morning, which is Day 1. Allow about 11–15 hours gate to gate. The clocks jump **7 hours ahead**, so Day 1 is for settling in, not sightseeing. The trip page's **Flights** section shows the airports, connection and times of the fare being tracked.

**Day 1 — Arrive.** See the **Duomo** square and the glass-roofed **Galleria
Vittorio Emanuele II**. Spin your heel on the bull mosaic for luck.

**Day 2 — The Last Supper, then the castle.** Your 15-minute slot at
**Leonardo's *Last Supper*** (under-18s free, but every person needs a
reservation). Then go to **Castello Sforzesco**, the Sforza dukes' castle.
It has moats, towers, an armoury, and Michelangelo's unfinished **Rondanini
Pietà**, the sculpture he was working on days before he died. The **Sempione
park** behind the castle has playgrounds.

**Day 3 — The Ambrosiana, then the cathedral roof.** The **Pinacoteca
Ambrosiana** holds Raphael's full-size **cartoon for *The School of Athens***
(Room 7). It also has Caravaggio's *Basket of Fruit*, Leonardo's *Portrait
of a Musician*, and pages of his notebooks, the *Codex Atlanticus*. In the
afternoon, take the lift to the **Duomo rooftop terraces** and walk among
the spires and statues.

**Day 4 — Leonardo's machines, then Brera, then the Roastery.** The
**National Museum of Science and Technology Leonardo da Vinci** has models
of his machines built from the notebooks. It also has a real **submarine**,
the *Enrico Toti*, which you can board on a tour (*check* ages). It's unique,
so it passes the profile's test. In the afternoon, **Pinacoteca di Brera**:
Raphael's *Marriage of the Virgin*, Mantegna's *Dead Christ* and
Caravaggio's *Supper at Emmaus*. End at the **Starbucks Reserve Roastery** in
Piazza Cordusio, the full roasting experience the profile asks for.

**Day 5 — Train to Varenna** (1 h). Walk up the old mule path to **Castello
di Vezio**. It's a medieval tower with **falconry**, with birds of prey
trained on site, and a view down the lake.

**Day 6 — Bellagio.** A 15-minute ferry crossing, short enough for a
non-boat family. Explore the stepped lanes and the gardens of **Villa
Melzi**. Swim at the **Lido di Varenna** in the afternoon.

**Day 7 — Villa Carlotta.** Short ferry to Tremezzo. It's sculpture and
gardens: Canova-school marble (*Cupid and Psyche*), Thorvaldsen, Hayez's
*The Last Kiss*, and terraced gardens with azaleas and a bamboo grove.

**Day 8 — Como and the Brunate funicular.** Take the train or ferry to
**Como**, ride the **funicular** up to **Brunate**, and walk to the
lighthouse for the whole-lake view. See the old walled town below.

**Day 9 — The lake, slow.** A beach and pool day. *Or* take the **Bernina
Express** day trip from Tirano over the Alps into Switzerland, the most
famous train ride in the Alps (long: about 2 h to Tirano first). In the afternoon, take the train to a hotel by **Malpensa airport** (about 2¼ h, changing at Milano Centrale), so the flight home is a short shuttle ride.

**Day 10 — Fly home from Malpensa.** The hotel shuttle to the terminal takes about 15 minutes; be there 3 hours before the flight. It's a full travel day home: westbound flights usually leave late morning or midday and land in St. Louis that evening, about 12–16 hours gate to gate. You gain back the 7 hours.

## Food — and protein for the vegetarian parent

- **Risotto alla milanese**; **pizzoccheri**, buckwheat pasta with cheese,
  potato and cabbage from the valleys above the lake; polenta with cheese.
- Plenty of vegetarian choice in Milan. **Joia** is the famous
  (Michelin-starred) vegetarian restaurant, for a parents' night.
- Lake fish for the others: perch (*pesce persico*) with butter and sage.
- **Kids' backup:** pasta, pizza, cotoletta (Milanese schnitzel).

**Coffee:** the **Starbucks Reserve Roastery** is on Day 4. **Beer:**
**Birrificio Lambrate**, Milan's first craft brewery, for the parents.

## Watch out for

- **Varenna is steep and stepped.** Pick an apartment near the station or
  the ferry, and bring a carrier for the 3-year-old.
- **The lake is busiest July–August.** June is calmer.
- **Ferries are crowded on weekends.** Do Bellagio and Villa Carlotta on
  weekdays.
