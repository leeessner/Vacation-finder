# What this family likes — the brief every plan is written against

From the family's own answers (October 2026). No names here on purpose:
this repository is public. Two parents, four children (8, 7, 5, 3).

## Art — quality above all
- Favourite period by far: the **Renaissance**. Favourite artist: **Raphael**;
  then **Michelangelo, Leonardo da Vinci, Bernini**.
- Also: **Baroque** — **Caravaggio, Rembrandt, Poussin** and their peers.
- **Great sculpture**, Renaissance to **Rodin**.
- Impressionism: interested, but not worth going out of the way for.
- Modern and contemporary: not interested.
- The famous museums, yes — but a small museum with first-rate work is just
  as good. Examples given: the Vatican Museums, the Galleria Colonna in Rome,
  the Prado, the National Gallery and Portrait Gallery in Washington.
- Historic sights count too: Tower of London, Trevi Fountain, Colosseum,
  Spanish Steps.

## Coffee — the experience, not the ritual
- A roastery tour (loved one in Kansas City); **Starbucks Reserve** in New
  York and Chicago was a highlight. Good coffee and a place that makes its
  own; nothing fussier than that.

## Beer
- **Belgian Trappist** is the favourite; **Bavarian beer gardens**; the big
  famous ones where it fits (the Hofbräuhaus in Munich); **microbreweries**
  generally. Ones that work with kids along, and ones for the two parents.

## Experiences that worked
- Walking old towns and poking into shops (Capri).
- A boat tour (not usually their thing, but it was great).
- **Local making-of experiences**: a Parmesan dairy in Italy, port wine in
  Portugal, a cooking class *if in Italy*. The point is the local culture.

## The kids
- **Castles** (huge), **animals**, **playgrounds**, **trains**, **beaches**
  that are different from home (the French Riviera, not another Gulf Coast).
- Science museums only if unique (an interactive Leonardo museum, yes; a
  generic one, no — St. Louis already has a great one).
- Not big on boats. Pretty adventurous overall.

## Food
- Local food wherever they are. **One parent is vegetarian** and struggled
  for protein in Italy — plans should name good vegetarian options.
- Fish on the coast for the others. Kids: pasta in Italy, burgers in London;
  they'll try things, but there must be a backup.

## Pace and staying
- One main thing in the morning; the afternoon can hold another. The
  3-year-old keeps up.
- Europe: walk and use transit in cities; a car only outside them.
- Resorts only as a **whole nature experience** — pool plus guided walks,
  mountains, outdoors (like the French and Italian examples), never just a
  pool. Active and outdoors, but not wilderness: no camping; a guide is
  welcome (e.g. a guided Black Forest walk).
- **Theme parks: out.** Disneyland Paris only as a single special day.
