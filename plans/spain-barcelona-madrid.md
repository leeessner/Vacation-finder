# Spain — Barcelona & Madrid — draft plan (summer 2027)

**Shape:** 10 nights. Five in Barcelona: Gaudí, a castle on a hill, a rack
railway to a mountain monastery, and the beach. Then the fast train (AVE) to
Madrid for five: the Prado, the Royal Palace's armoury, and a fairy-tale
castle in Segovia. It's walking, metro and trains the whole way; no car.

Matches the tracked trip **"Spain — Barcelona & Madrid"**. Written against
`plans/family-profile.md`. Prices were checked in October 2026 from the
sources at the end; anything not confirmed is marked *check*. Euros are

## Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive. The beach.** Settle in, then spend the late afternoon at
**Barceloneta** with paddling and sandcastles. Spain eats late, so an early
tapas dinner is fine.

**Day 2 — Sagrada Família, then Park Güell.** Take the first slot at the
**Sagrada Família**. **Children under 11 are free**, so only the parents pay.
Inside, the stained glass turns the stone forest blue and orange. In the
afternoon, **Park Güell**: the mosaic dragon, the gingerbread houses and the
long wavy bench (timed tickets).

**Day 3 — Montjuïc: cable car and castle.** Ride the **cable car** up to
**Montjuïc Castle**, a star-shaped fortress with cannons and harbour views.
On the way down, the Olympic stadium and the gardens. Back in town, the
**Magic Fountain** shows run on summer evenings (*check*; they were paused
during the 2024 drought).

**Day 4 — Gothic Quarter and La Boqueria.** Wander the **Gothic Quarter's**
lanes to the **cathedral**. Its cloister keeps 13 white geese, a
centuries-old tradition (a bonus). Then go to **La Boqueria** market for
fruit cups and a counter lunch. Spend the afternoon at the beach or the pool.

**Day 5 — Montserrat by rack railway.** Take the train an hour out, then the
**rack railway** (cremallera) up the serrated mountain to the **monastery**.
The boys' choir, one of Europe's oldest, sings in the basilica around 1 pm on
most days (*check* the schedule). Funiculars climb higher for short, easy
mountain walks. It's the trains day of the trip.

**Day 6 — AVE to Madrid** (about 2½–3 h). In the afternoon, go to **Retiro
Park**: the Crystal Palace, playgrounds, and puppet shows on weekends.

**Day 7 — The Prado.** Arrive at opening and stay about 2½ hours. **Under-18s
are free.** See Raphael's *Holy Family* ("La Perla") and *Portrait of a
Cardinal*, Velázquez's *Las Meninas* (ask the kids to find the painter in
the picture), Caravaggio's *David with the Head of Goliath*, Rubens, Goya,
and Bosch's *Garden of Earthly Delights*, which kids can study for ages.
Have **chocolate con churros at San Ginés** after.

**Day 8 — The Royal Palace and its armoury.** Europe's largest royal palace
by floor area. The **Royal Armoury** has suits of armour for kings, children
and horses, and is the castle-lover's room of Madrid. Then the **Plaza
Mayor** and the **Mercado de San Miguel** for lunch.

**Day 9 — Segovia.** Take the AVE (27 min). Stand under the 2,000-year-old
Roman **aqueduct**, then go to the **Alcázar**, the turreted castle on a
cliff that looks straight out of a storybook. Climb the tower for the view.

**Day 10 — Madrid, slow.** Choose: the **Thyssen** (Caravaggio, Holbein's
*Henry VIII*, Ghirlandaio's *Giovanna Tornabuoni*), or a lazy pool and park
day.

**Day 11 — Fly home** from MAD.

## Options

- **Toledo instead of Segovia** (AVE, 33 min). A walled hilltop city, with El
  Greco and a sword-making tradition.
- **Tibidabo's funicular** for the view over Barcelona. The amusement park at
  the top is skipped, since theme parks are out.

## Food — and protein for the vegetarian parent

- **Tortilla española** (potato and egg omelette) is everywhere, and the
  kids eat it too. Add **garbanzos con espinacas** (chickpeas with spinach),
  **pimientos de padrón**, Manchego cheese and **pan con tomate**.
- **Barcelona:** **Teresa Carles** is a well-known vegetarian restaurant.
- **Others:** seafood paella, grilled fish, *jamón*.
- **Kids' backup:** croquetas, tortilla, pasta, and churros as the reward.

**Beer:** Barcelona's **Fàbrica Moritz**, the city's historic brewery, now a
bar. Madrid has a growing craft-beer scene in Malasaña.

## Watch out for

- **Dinner is at 9 pm in Spain.** Plan a big late lunch and an early tapas
  supper, which suits the kids better.
- **Heat:** Madrid in July is 35°C+. Do the outdoor sights before noon, and
  keep the pool or a museum for the afternoon.
- **Pickpockets** on the Barcelona metro and Las Ramblas.
